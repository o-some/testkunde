TESTKUNDE / DEMO WEBSITE

Accessibility

This demo was designed to work across devices and with a keyboard. Full compliance with WCAG, BITV or BFSG is not claimed without comprehensive assessment.

Implemented features

  • Semantic sections, page-language identification and a skip link to main content.
  • Image alternatives, labelled fields and visible focus indicators.
  • Keyboard-operable filters, FAQ and registration dialog. Escape closes the dialog.
  • Responsive layout and support for the “reduce motion” system setting.
  • No autoplay videos or flashing content. Both scrolling text bands can be paused and become static when reduced motion is enabled.

Limitations and feedback

No complete assessment with every assistive technology has been performed. Before launch, screen-reader behaviour, zoom and contrast should be reviewed again with final content. A real accessibility contact will be added with the operator profile.

Legal classification

Whether Germany's Accessibility Strengthening Act or other rules apply to a real offer depends on the service and actual operator. This page is not a legally audited accessibility statement.
